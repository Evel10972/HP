Production process (may vary by commission platform)

  1. Payment (depending on the platform)
  2. Discuss the request
  3. Create and present a rough sketch (for DM requests, an advance payment follows)
  4. Finish the artwork
  5. Final review
  6. Delivery (and any remaining payment)

If you have a preferred delivery date, please let me know before placing your request. I will review the work and schedule, then tell you what deadline I can meet.

Production and revisions

As a rule, I do not offer retakes or major revisions.

If the result differs significantly from your request because I misunderstood it, I will make corrections where possible.

Please explain your preferences in detail during our discussion so I can create an illustration close to your vision.

Major changes to the pose, outfit, or composition after the final drawing may incur an extra fee or may not be possible.

Delivery formats

  • JPG
  • PNG
  • PSD (Photoshop)

Please tell me your preferred image size and file format when you make your request.

About your request

Please provide character reference sheets or images if you have them.

Even if your idea is not fully formed, describing the following details as clearly as you can will help me create the illustration you want:

  • Pose
  • Outfit
  • Accessories
  • Colors
  • Gender
  • Species
  • Mood
  • Other preferences

When purchasing or discussing a commission, please also provide:

  • Your character concept
  • Intended use
  • Preferred delivery date
  • Image size
  • Preferred file format
  • Other requests

Important notes

  • Do not claim my work as your own.
  • Redistribution or sale without permission is prohibited.
  • I may share completed illustrations as examples of my work on my portfolio or social media.
  • I may decline requests that violate public standards or raise rights issues.
  • Copyright is not transferred.

Please feel free to contact me if you have any questions!

DM requests and payment

For requests made by DM, payment is accepted through PayPal only. If payment through a manager or another party outside the DM is requested, if I am asked to pay an additional amount myself, or if any other unnecessary action is demanded, I will end the discussion immediately due to fraud concerns.
